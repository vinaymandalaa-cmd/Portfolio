Fold 02 / the move

Spaces first. Screens after.

I grew up in Karimnagar, Telangana and moved to Hyderabad for design school, the city gave me scale, competition and the first rooms I ever designed for someone else's money.

I studied interior design at JNAFAU and spent four years learning how physical space moves people: where they enter, what they see first, where they slow down. Then I realised the thing I actually wanted to move people through was an interface.

Fold 05 / how I work

Strong opinions,
loosely held.

Everyone in the room sees the product from somewhere I can't stand, engineering knows the cost, sales knows the objection, support knows the complaint. I want those opinions early and I take them seriously, including the ones I disagree with.

Then I decide, and I say why: what the data showed, what users did, what my gut says when the data is thin. I'll argue for an idea until it earns its place and drop it the moment the evidence says it shouldn't exist, both have happened on projects on this site. Fast to learn, easy to correct, hard to bore.
